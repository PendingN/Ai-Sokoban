## Export notes

Texture outputs were resized to the dimensions in README.md. The wall-front
strip uses the brick region (273, 182, 661, 360) of the 2172 × 724 source.
Transparent sprites were cropped to their content, resampled with premultiplied
alpha and padded onto shared canvases. Boxes share a bottom anchor at y=156;
players share a bottom anchor at y=162. Artwork was not redrawn by the renderer.
